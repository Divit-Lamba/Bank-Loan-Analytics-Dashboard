# 💼 Bank Loan Analytics Dashboard  

### 📊 Description  
Comprehensive Bank Loan Analytics Project showcasing SQL-based KPI tracking, data visualization, and domain insights. Includes query documentation, terminology explanations, and dashboards analyzing loan performance, borrower profiles, and portfolio health for data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📁 Project Overview  
This project focuses on analyzing **bank loan data** to assess lending performance, borrower health, and portfolio risk.  
It combines **SQL queries, business understanding, and KPI dashboards** to generate actionable insights for financial decision-making.  

---

## 🧩 Problem Statement  
The goal is to analyze and monitor key loan metrics to help banks track performance, identify risk, and understand borrower behavior.  

### **Key Objectives**
- Evaluate lending performance through structured KPIs.  
- Differentiate between good and bad loans.  
- Visualize monthly, regional, and borrower-level insights.  
- Build a user-friendly dashboard for portfolio monitoring.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| **KPI Category** | **Metrics** |
|------------------|-------------|
| **Overall Performance** | Total Loan Applications, Total Funded Amount, Total Amount Received, Average Interest Rate, Average DTI |
| **Monthly Analysis** | Month-to-Date (MTD) and Month-over-Month (MoM) trends for all key metrics |
| **Loan Quality Metrics** | Good Loan % and Count, Bad Loan % and Count, Funded Amount, and Received Amount for both segments |
| **Loan Status Grid** | Breakdown by Loan Status showing applications, funded amounts, received amounts, Avg. Interest Rate, and Avg. DTI |

---

## 🗂️ Dashboards Overview  

### **Dashboard 1: KPI Summary**  
- Displays total loan applications, funded and received amounts.  
- Includes MTD and MoM performance metrics.  
- Compares Good vs Bad loan performance.  

### **Dashboard 2: Overview**
- **Monthly Trends (Line Chart):** Seasonal patterns in lending.  
- **Regional Analysis (Map):** State-wise distribution of loans.  
- **Loan Term Analysis (Donut Chart):** Breakdown by repayment duration.  
- **Employee Length (Bar Chart):** Relation between employment history and loans.  
- **Loan Purpose (Bar Chart):** Primary reasons for borrowing.  
- **Home Ownership (Tree Map):** Impact of ownership status on loan approvals.  

### **Dashboard 3: Details**
- A consolidated grid view of all borrower and loan-related data.  
- Provides a snapshot of key financial metrics and borrower profiles.  
- Enables efficient tracking of loan performance.

## 🧠 Domain Knowledge and Terminologies  

Each field in the dataset has been explained in detail to clarify its business relevance and how banks use it.  

Examples:
- **Loan ID:** Unique identifier for each loan which helps in tracking lifecycle.  
- **Address State:** Borrower’s region used for risk assessment.  
- **Employee Length:** Indicates job stability.  
- **Grade & Subgrade:** Reflect credit risk levels.  
- **DTI (Debt-to-Income Ratio):** Measures borrower’s debt capacity.  
- **Interest Rate:** Annual cost of borrowing.  
- **Loan Amount:** Total principal borrowed.  

📘 *Full terminology explanation included in the “Terminologies_Document.md” file.*
